The battery's label can place its state of charge where you want it with `battery.state_of_charge_position`: under the power as before (`below_power`, the default), swapped with the power so the charge is the large value (`above_power`), or on the power's own line, after it (`after_power`) or before it (`before_power`). It is also in the battery page of the UI editor.
